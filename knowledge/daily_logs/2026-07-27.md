## Remix target: Zapp — "More Bounce to the Ounce" (1980, Warner Bros)

- **On-brand why:** Roger Troutman/Zapp is P-Funk-lineage low-end royalty
  — the exact talk-box, sub-heavy, groove-over-everything DNA that
  Chicago/Detroit house was built on top of. Funky *and* sexy in the
  literal sense the title promises, squarely 70s/80s/90s crate-dig lane,
  and "bounce" as a theme rhymes directly with this week's donk-bounce
  moment without being a donk track.
- **Mash/edit approach:** isolate the iconic bass hook as a 2–4 bar loop,
  run it through a low-pass filter-house intro (sweep it open over 8
  bars), then let your own kick/hat groove lock in underneath at 122–124
  BPM once the filter opens — treat the original bass as a guest, not the
  foundation, and layer a warm sub underneath it in C minor to keep it
  inside the "1998" fingerprint. Hook-only edit, not a full re-record —
  same restraint as the Candi Staton and EWF flips logged the last two
  weeks, keeps it a personal/preview edit rather than a bootleg.
- **Trending potential:** the "bounce" framing gives it a built-in caption
  hook riding the Morning Dew (Donk) curiosity wave, while the sample
  itself is recognizable enough on its own merits (still a beloved
  funk/hip-hop sample staple) to work even after that news cycle fades.
