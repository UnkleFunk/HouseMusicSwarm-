## Remix target: Marvin Gaye — "Sexual Healing" (1982, Columbia)

- **On-brand why:** it's one of the most famous drum-machine-driven soul
  records ever made (Roland CR-78, programmed by Gaye himself) — the
  "dusty drum machines" lineage in the taste profile isn't just a 909/808
  ideal, this is its direct ancestor. Minor-key-adjacent, slow-burning,
  sub-heavy, unmistakably sexy without being crude — squarely the
  70s/80s/90s crate-dig lane and a title that does the "funky or sexy"
  brief literally.
- **Mash/edit approach:** isolate the CR-78 clave/rim pattern and the
  "healing, healing" vocal ad-lib as a 2-bar loop, run a slow filter-house
  open over 8 bars, then drop your own kick/hat groove and a warm C-minor
  sub underneath once the filter opens — treat the original drum machine
  as a guest texture layered against your own low end, not a full
  replacement. Hook-only edit at 122–124 BPM, same restraint used on the
  Candi Staton, EWF, and Zapp flips logged the last three weeks — keeps it
  a personal/preview edit, not a bootleg.
- **Trending potential:** rides directly into trend #1 above — a warm,
  filtered, groove-forward flip of an instantly-recognizable "sexy" soul
  classic is exactly the sound currently landing in the summer-soundtrack
  discovery pool, with a title/hook recognizable enough to travel on its
  own merits after any specific news cycle fades.
