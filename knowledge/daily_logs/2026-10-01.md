**1. Anita Ward "Ring My Bell" (1979) is a top Instagram Reels audio** (outfit /
feminine-lifestyle videos, Buffer Sept 2026 roundup). A 70s disco-funk record
is carrying mainstream Reels right now, so four-on-the-floor + bell/clav
percussion is already in casual ears.
- **How to use it:** in the nearest-to-finished 75 Deep track, add a dry
  percussive "bell/cowbell + clav stab" accent on the off-beat in the
  breakdown (own sound, no sample). Cut a 15s Reel of just that groove

**2. Instagram's "Process" trend (October)** — creators show tools, steps and
behind-the-scenes work, then reveal the finished result. This is the 75 Deep

  late-night build) for one track; open with the empty project / clock time,

  is the sound. One post per near-finished track.

Minor note: seasonal fall/cozy edits are everywhere — a warm, low-lit "late
night, autumn" visual treatment fits the 75 Deep mood for free.

## Remix target: Cheryl Lynn — "Got to Be Real" (1978, Columbia)

- **On-brand why:** funky, sexy, soul-powered disco with a rolling bassline
  and a huge vocal; direct ancestor of house vocal tradition. Not previously
  used in the EWF / Candi Staton / Zapp edits, so it widens the catalog.
- **Mash/edit approach:** lift the ad-lib "got to be real" vocal phrase and a
  2-bar bass/guitar pocket; chop the vocal into a call-and-response hook over
  your own kick/hat at 122–124 BPM, filter the original bass out and replace
  with your warm sub in the track's key. Hook-only personal/preview edit.
- **Trending potential:** rides the same 70s-disco-on-Reels wave as "Ring My
  Bell" and the "feminine lifestyle/outfit" audio niche; the vocal hook is
  instantly recognizable and loops cleanly for 15s clips.
