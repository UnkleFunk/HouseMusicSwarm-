## Remix target: Teena Marie — "Square Biz" (1981, Gordy/Motown)

- **On-brand why:** gritty, bass-forward funk with a chantable, cheeky
  hook and a sultry-but-street vocal; sexy and funky without being the
  obvious pick. Not yet used in prior scout picks (Candi Staton, EWF
  "Fantasy", Aretha "Rock Steady") and far less sampled than Loleatta or
  Chaka, so more headroom.
- **Mash/edit approach:** loop the bass + drum pocket as an 8-bar
  filter-house intro, chop the "square biz" vocal as a rhythmic stab on
  the off-beat, tuck a 909 clap and shuffled hats under it at 122-124 BPM,
  one drop, hook-only personal/preview edit (same release-safe restraint
  as earlier flips). Verify key/tempo against the actual record before
  chopping; I did not audio-check it.
- **Trending potential:** rides the still-running vintage soul/funk edit
  wave logged in July, and the hook phrase is short and caption-friendly
  for the vinyl-find -> DAW-flip reaction format. Honest caveat: no
  evidence it is trending today; the bet is on the wave, not the song.
