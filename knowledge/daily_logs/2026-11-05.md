## Remix target: Cheryl Lynn — "Got to Be Real" (1978, Columbia)

- **On-brand why:** Disco-funk with a Louis Johnson-style rubbery bassline and

  current edits.
- **Mash/edit approach:** Take the 4-bar bass-and-clap intro and the "got to
  be real" vocal tag. Run the bassline through a low-pass sweep over 8 bars
  at 122-124 BPM (time-stretch from ~118), drop your own kick and hats
  underneath, and keep the original vocal for the breakdown only. Add a warm
  C-minor sub under the original bass. Hook-only preview edit, not a
  bootleg release.
- **Trending potential:** the Ramalama wave shows disco-lineage vocals
  are landing with a TikTok audience right now. "Got to Be Real" is
  instantly recognizable, and its title works as a caption hook ("keep it
  real at 1am").
