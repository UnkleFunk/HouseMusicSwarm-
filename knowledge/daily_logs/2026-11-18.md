## Two trends worth chasing

**1. House/electronic is quietly all over TikTok's lifestyle lane.**
Metricool's weekly list has Star Slinger "Too Self Aware" (deep house/UKG/hip-hop,
used for outfit reveals + playful lifestyle clips), LEL "Take Me Down" (house-pop,
travel/aesthetic edits), Sinny & 7vvch "Time Back" (slow-build techno, mood/cinematic
transitions) and Mark Allan Wolfe "Self Aware" (instrumental; night-sky shots, vlogs,
room makeovers). "Self aware" is literally the theme.
- **How to use it:** the 75 Deep story IS a self-aware night routine. Cut a 15-20s
  vertical of the most nearly-finished track — the filter-sweep / drum-and-bass
  intro only — over a "what I do with the 75 minutes after everyone's asleep" clip
  (studio lights, Ableton arrangement view, the clock). Caption: "self-aware
  enough to know I should be asleep." Slow-build intros match the "Time Back"
  transition usage.

**2. Warm jazz/soul as the "personal, emotional" sound.**
#1 on Metricool is a jazz track (Noah Preminger & Max Light, "Sunset in Girona")
and Teddy Swims "What More Can I Say" (bluesy R&B) is #9 — people are reaching
for warm, human, slightly nostalgic textures.
- **How to use it:** put a soulful chord stab/Rhodes or vocal chop in the first
  4 bars of a 75 Deep teaser instead of the drop — lead with the warmth, reveal
  the groove at bar 5. Fits the "reclaimed late-night time" feeling without
  sounding like a club edit.

(Also noted: Brazilian funk "Montagem da Chupeta" at 1M videos, +29K/day. Not our
lane sonically, but its stutter-bounce drum pattern is a possible hat/perc
rhythm borrow — low priority.)

## Remix target: Change — "The Glow of Love" (1980, Warner/Atlantic)

(Prior weeks used Candi Staton, EWF "Fantasy", Zapp — this is a fresh pick.)
- **On-brand:** Italo-disco-meets-NYC-funk with a Luther Vandross lead vocal;
  sleek, sexy, late-night, deeply loved by DJs and a Chicago/NY house staple
  lineage. Funky bass + glossy synths sit inside the Unkle Funk fingerprint.
- **Quick edit approach:** isolate the "...the glow of love" vocal hook plus the
  bass motif as 4-8 bar loops. Open with a filtered pad/vocal-only intro (matches
  the warm-intro trend above), bring in your kick/hats at ~120-122 BPM (check the
  original's tempo and time-stretch lightly), and let your own sub sit under the
  original bassline. Hook-only edit; keep it as a personal/preview edit, not a
  public bootleg (clearance).
- **Trending potential:** a smooth soulful vocal over a slow-build house intro
  fits both current lanes (house lifestyle edits + warm R&B/soul), and the
  "glow" phrase makes an easy caption hook ("late-night glow").
