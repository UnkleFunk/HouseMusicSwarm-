## Two trends worth chasing

**1. GTA VI hype wave: "Flower In A Hurricane" (Tame Impala, SZA, Justice)**
- #1 sound on TikTok (658 new videos in 7 days) and #1 on Reels (281). The GTA San Andreas theme, "GTA 6" fan songs, and "Grand Trance Audio (San AndDreams)" (~200K videos each, +17-20K/day) are all in the top 5-10 growing.
- Justice is on the biggest sound of the week. That puts French-touch / filtered disco-house in front of a mass audience.
- The "Vice City / San Andreas" mood (neon, 80s radio, night driving) is a close match for the 75 Deep late-night mood.
- **How to use it:** take the nearest-finished 75 Deep track and cut a 15s "night drive" clip. Put a filtered, side-chained chord loop over a four-on-floor groove with a warm, slightly saturated 80s-radio-style top end. Skip the sound itself and caption it "city at 2am, the only hour that's mine." The sound can't be sampled and the captions can stay generic. Both give you the GTA-adjacent search traffic without leaning on the tracks.

**2. Format: "In this town everybody goes out partying except me" (17 posts, new)**
- A "staying in while everyone's out" format. It is small and new, but it is the inverse of 75 Deep: you are not missing the party, because the 2am hours are the party.
- **How to use it:** film the same format but flip the punchline. The studio is lit up, everyone else is asleep, and the groove drops on the cut. Use a 4-8 bar funky loop with the vocal chop or horn stab from whichever track has one. Caption: "everybody's out partying. I'm out here finishing the record."

## Remix target: Change — "Searching" (1980, Atlantic/WEA)

- **On-brand why:** Italo-produced disco-funk with a young Luther Vandross lead, about as soulful and sexy as 1980 got. The bass-and-clap groove is in the same family as the Chicago house roots. It also fits the Vice City / early-80s radio mood that GTA VI is bringing back.
- **Mash/edit approach:** loop the first two bars of the vocal hook and pitch to key (target 122-124 BPM, around C minor). Run it through a low-pass sweep over 8 bars, then drop your own kick/hat and a warm sub underneath. Treat the original as a guest sample, not the foundation. Keep it to a hook-only preview edit, same restraint as the Candi Staton / EWF / Zapp flips in earlier logs.
- **Trending potential:** a GTA-radio-era sound with a recognizable vocal. A 15s "this is what Vice City sounds like in 2026" Reel is an easy hook for it. Disco/funk revivals have also been running on Reels (Anita Ward and EWF "September" in September roundups).
- **Rights note:** personal/preview edit only. A public release needs clearance.
